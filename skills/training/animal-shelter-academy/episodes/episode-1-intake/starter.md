# Getting Started

Open the Animal Shelter workspace in prooph board.

Navigate to:

Season 1 → Episode 1 → New Arrival

Locate the event:

➡️ Animal Arrived At Shelter

Interview the available stakeholders.

Recommended first question:

"Sarah, can you walk me through what happens when a stray animal arrives at the shelter?"

Ask `Who can I interview?` if you need help identifying who can answer questions.

Ask `What are the objectives of this episode?` to review the goals of this episode.

Ask `What artifacts are available?` to get a hint which artifacts should be discovered.

Remember:

The objective is not to guess the reference model.

The objective is to discover how the business actually works.
